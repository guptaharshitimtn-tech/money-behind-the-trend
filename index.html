  • Insured farmerCovered

In 2026, ₹0 premiumbuys relief, not a claim.

"India's insurance gap follows the premium, not the risk. We usually blame awareness. Andhra just ran the experiment on price."

Andhra insured 31.92 lakh farmers in 2024, when the state paid their share of the crop premium. It stopped in 2025 and enrolment fell 77%, to 7.43 lakh. Same farmers, same cyclone coast. Then Arnab left a ₹4,600 crore bill, and Swiss Re estimates 93% of India's catastrophe exposure is uninsured. Not advice. Just a note on who's holding the storm.
