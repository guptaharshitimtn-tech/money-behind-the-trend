Who holds the bill

Ramayana.₹4,000 crore, two films.

  • DNEGBilled
  • The streamerSigned early
  • The lenders₹4,138 cr debt
  • ShareholdersWaiting

At the box office, a gamble.At DNEG, an invoice.

"Everyone is asking if Ramayana can earn back ₹4,000 crore. Part of it came back before the first show."

Ramayana opened to ₹128.75 crore worldwide, level with Adipurush's ₹128.08 crore. The VFX was done by DNEG, 88.3% owned by Prime Focus, the producer's listed company, whose deck calls this a "natural hedge." The risk sits with lenders: ₹515 crore of interest in FY26, more than the film's reported ₹450 crore streaming deal. The real review is the 13 November results. Not advice. Just how I read a box office headline.
